Vigilia Nezo · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Who we are

Vigilia Nezo ("the App") is a private tool used only by the creator Nezo Secreto to publish his own content to his own social media accounts and read the statistics of that content. It has no other users.

2. What data the App uses

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, Google (YouTube), Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and X after the owner logs in. They allow the App to act only on the owner's own accounts.
  • Basic account information of the owner's accounts (for example, account name and ID), to know where to publish.
  • The owner's own videos and images, which are uploaded to the owner's accounts.
  • Statistics of the owner's own posts (views, watch time, reach, followers gained), to measure how each post performs.

The App does not collect data about other people, does not read messages, and does not access followers' personal information.

3. Where the data is stored

Everything is stored locally, on the owner's own computer. There is no server and no external database. Access tokens are kept in a private folder that is not shared or published.

4. How the data is used

Only to publish the owner's content to the owner's accounts and to show the owner how that content performs. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

5. Data sent to platforms

When publishing, the App sends the video or image and its caption to the platform chosen by the owner (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or X), through that platform's official API. Each platform processes it under its own privacy policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the App. The owner can revoke access at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Meta: Settings > Apps and websites (Facebook) / Business integrations (Instagram).

After revoking, the tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner's computer.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
